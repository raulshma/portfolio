The media stack, made portable.

UniArr consolidates the individual services used to discover, request, and organize media into one cohesive mobile interface.

The product treats offline support as part of the core workflow, so users can keep an eye on releases and make changes without depending on a perfect connection.

One control surface for a sprawling setup.

Service integrations are organized around the everyday tasks of finding media, following releases, and keeping a library in shape.

01

Unified media hub

Brings Sonarr, Radarr, Jellyseerr, and other media services into a common mobile workflow instead of a collection of disconnected dashboards.

02

Release-aware management

Supports discovery and management flows that make it easier to follow a library as new content moves through the stack.

03

Offline-first thinking

Robust local state keeps essential information and intended actions available when the network is not.

Technology

Native motion, focused state.

A TypeScript-based React Native stack makes the product portable while keeping its navigation, local state, and service interactions deliberate.
