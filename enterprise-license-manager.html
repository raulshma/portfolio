One lifecycle, from purchase through renewal.

This backend system brings the essential stages of a license lifecycle into one operational flow: purchase, activation, and renewal.

Stripe supports payments while Xero receives the related accounting sync, connecting commercial activity with the systems that need to reconcile it.

What the system supports

Commercial workflows without disconnected handoffs.

Purchase and activation

License purchase and activation are handled as connected stages of the same backend workflow.

Renewal operations

Renewal support keeps ongoing licenses part of a deliberate lifecycle rather than a separate process.

Financial integrations

Stripe payments and automated Xero accounting sync connect the product to its financial operations.
